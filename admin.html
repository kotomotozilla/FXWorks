Costs

What each contract costs: the agreed price of its sub-contracts plus what has been reported against it. Sub-contracts count at their price — the commitment — not at what has been reported so far. Reports count once accepted; those submitted and not yet accepted are shown separately.

By contract

Work done for it — everything reported against this contract through its projects, by everyone who worked on it. Earned under it — what this counterparty reported under their own contract, wherever the work was done. The two are different views of the same reports, so they are not added together. Cost = sub-contracts + work done for it; the last column is what is left of the budget on a contract we pay, or the margin on one we are paid.

A contract as a set of clauses. An annex / SOW adds to the agreement (the same topic in both is detail, not conflict); an amendment replaces the clause it restates, matched by clause number.
